== Release control demo C: manual rollback to an older commit, then forward again
$ curl /version   (before)
{"version":"27464722832d","policy_version":0,"llm_provider":"scripted"}

$ helm rollback clinic-agent 2 -n clinic --wait      (revision 2 = image 3be1ef34d67b)
Rollback was a success! Happy Helming!
$ curl /version
{"version":"3be1ef34d67b","policy_version":0,"llm_provider":"scripted"}

$ helm rollback clinic-agent 5 -n clinic --wait      (back to the current release, image 27464722832d)
Rollback was a success! Happy Helming!
$ curl /version
{"version":"27464722832d","policy_version":0,"llm_provider":"scripted"}
