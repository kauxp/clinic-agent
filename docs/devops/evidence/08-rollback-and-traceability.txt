== Release control demo C: manual rollback to an older commit, then forward again
$ curl /version   (before)
{"version":"81ff8d5c9fdc","policy_version":0,"llm_provider":"scripted"}

$ helm rollback clinic-agent 1 -n clinic --wait      (revision 1 = image 5fc2b3acfd56)
Rollback was a success! Happy Helming!
$ curl /version
{"version":"5fc2b3acfd56","policy_version":0,"llm_provider":"scripted"}

$ helm rollback clinic-agent 9 -n clinic --wait      (back to the current release, image 81ff8d5c9fdc)
Rollback was a success! Happy Helming!
$ curl /version
{"version":"81ff8d5c9fdc","policy_version":0,"llm_provider":"scripted"}
